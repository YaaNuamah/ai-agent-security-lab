# Learning Journal

## Entry Template

### YYYY-MM-DD — Topic

**What I explored**  
The question, tool, concept, or activity examined.

**What I learned**  
The clearest new technical or operational insight.

**What surprised me**  
An assumption that changed, a constraint discovered, or an unexpected result.

**How it changes the Agent Lab**  
The decision, improvement, or new question produced by the learning.

**Next step**
